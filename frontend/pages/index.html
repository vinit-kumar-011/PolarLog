About PolarLog

India runs three permanent polar research stations — two in Antarctica and one in the Arctic. PolarLog replaces fragmented spreadsheets and manual logs across NCPOR, shipping contractors, and Navy/Coast Guard with one shared operating picture.

Why it existsResupply windows are short and weather-locked — a missed manifest or lost asset can mean waiting a full season for the next ship.
Who uses itNCPOR logistics staff, station personnel, and transport contractors, each with role-based access to the same live data.
What's nextOffline-first sync over Iridium/VSAT, AI cargo-weight optimization, and ML-based consumable-depletion forecasting.
