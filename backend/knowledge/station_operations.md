# Station Operations

## Station profiles

**Bharati** — Larsemann Hills, East Antarctica. Established 2012.
Year-round. Capacity 47. Primary focus: oceanography, geology.

**Maitri** — Schirmacher Oasis, East Antarctica. Established 1989.
Year-round. Capacity 25. Primary focus: geology, atmospheric science.

**Himadri** — Ny-Alesund, Svalbard, Arctic. Established 2008.
Seasonally staffed. Capacity 8. Primary focus: atmospheric science,
glaciology, aurora studies.

**Himansh** — Chandra basin, Lahaul-Spiti, Himalaya. Established 2016.
Summer only. Capacity 15. Primary focus: glaciology, hydrology.

## Operational status meanings

- **Operational** — fully staffed and functioning normally.
- **Under maintenance** — running, but one or more critical systems are
  being serviced. Reduced capacity.
- **Closed** — seasonally shut. No personnel, no resupply.

## Communication windows

Antarctic stations use satellite links with scheduled daily windows.
Himadri has continuous connectivity. Himansh relies on satellite phone
outside the summer season.
