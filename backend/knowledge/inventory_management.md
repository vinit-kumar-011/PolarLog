# Inventory Management

## Reorder levels

Every inventory item carries a reorder level — the quantity at which a
resupply request should be raised. It accounts for lead time, consumption
rate, and a safety margin.

Reorder levels are reviewed at the start of each expedition season.

## Stock health

- **Healthy** — above reorder level.
- **Low** — at or below reorder level but above 40% of it.
- **Critical** — at or below 40% of reorder level.

## Days remaining

Estimated days remaining is current quantity divided by daily usage rate.

This is an estimate. Actual consumption varies with weather, occupancy, and
operational tempo. Winter fuel consumption at Antarctic stations can run
50 to 80% above the summer average.

## Categories

- **Fuel** — diesel, aviation fuel, kerosene. Highest priority by volume.
- **Food** — dry rations, frozen goods, fresh produce.
- **Medical** — medical kits, pharmaceuticals, oxygen.
- **Equipment** — generator spares, vehicle parts, scientific instruments.
