# Resupply Protocol

## Critical fuel levels

When any fuel item at a station falls below its reorder level, the station
officer must raise a resupply request within 24 hours.

If fuel falls below 40% of the reorder level, the situation is classified
as CRITICAL. A critical fuel shortage requires:

1. Immediate notification to the station coordinator
2. An emergency resupply request, marked high priority
3. Daily consumption reporting until the shipment arrives
4. A consumption reduction plan if estimated days remaining is under 10

## Resupply lead times

Lead times vary by station and season.

- Bharati and Maitri (Antarctica): 21 to 45 days. No resupply is possible
  during the austral winter, roughly March to October.
- Himadri (Arctic, Svalbard): 10 to 14 days. Year-round access via Longyearbyen.
- Himansh (Himalayan, Chandra basin): 5 to 7 days in summer. The station is
  unmanned in winter and resupply is suspended.

Because Antarctic stations have the longest lead times and a hard seasonal
cutoff, their reorder levels are set substantially higher than Arctic or
Himalayan stations for the same item.

## Priority ordering

When several resupply requests compete for the same transport:

1. Medical supplies
2. Fuel below critical threshold
3. Food below reorder level
4. Fuel below reorder but above critical
5. Equipment and spares
